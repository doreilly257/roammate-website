# Kratie, Pakse and Savannakhet: editorial reference notes

Research checked 12 September 2026. These are source and licence notes, not task tracking.
All three guides use one three-day itinerary and `generateDerivedPages: false`.
Budgets are September 2026 editorial estimates, not supplier quotes; the Laos city-day budgets explicitly exclude regional excursions and private drivers.

## Kratie

- [Cambodian government news agency, February 2025](https://akp.gov.kh/post/detail/327643) identifies Kampi and Koh Trong as destinations and documents regional bamboo sticky rice and pomelos. No population estimate is repeated as current or converted into a sighting guarantee.
- [IWC responsible viewing handbook](https://wwhandbook.iwc.int/en/preparing-for-a-trip/what-is-responsible-whale-watching) informs no-chasing, no-feeding and patient-observation advice. These are recommendations, not a claim every operator follows a single international distance rule.
- [CRDT Koh Trong project](https://www.crdt.org.kh/post/crdt-completes-waste-management-project) is the source for waste-management context.
- FCDO Cambodia safety, health and entry pages and the official eVisa portal are linked in the guide. Border restrictions are geographically scoped and dated; visa eligibility and crossings require current checks.
- The three-day plan separates island cycling from Kampi, does not combine a long paddle with an ambitious temple circuit, and assumes full days after arrival.

## Pakse

- [Wat Phou Travels, a local operator](https://www.watphou-travels.com/pakse-city) supports the Wat Luang, Dao Heuang market and Phou Salao locations; no operator tariff or guaranteed hours is repeated.
- [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/481/) identifies Vat Phou as part of the wider Champasak cultural landscape. The guide explicitly distinguishes it from Pakse’s Phou Salao and reserves a separate excursion day.
- [Champasak tourism access page](https://tourismchampasak.com/access-to-champasak/) supplies road/air context, not a promise of current flight schedules; dated services must be checked with Lao Airlines.
- FCDO Laos health guidance supports the anthrax precaution for cattle and buffalo in the Pakse area. Entry information is labelled passport-specific, and outdoor/road precautions link to current advice.

## Savannakhet

- [Savannakhet tourism](https://www.tourismsavannakhet.org/) supports old-town architecture and murals.
- [Dinosaur Museum](https://www.tourismsavannakhet.org/dinosaur-museum) contradicts itself: Monday–Friday 9–4 and Monday–Saturday split hours appear on the same page. The guide deliberately gives no fixed hours or price, recommends reconfirmation and includes a backup activity.
- [Around Savannakhet](https://www.tourismsavannakhet.org/around-savannakhet) supports That Ing Hang and Beung Va as nearby but separate destinations. The guide does not copy the site’s ambitious motorcycle-loop recommendation.
- The tourism transport page is not evidence of a currently operating daily flight. Flights, borders and museum access remain explicitly conditional.
