# AEO Plan — roammate

**Date:** 2026-03-11

**What is AEO:** Answer Engine Optimization — ensuring roammate surfaces when users ask AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, Gemini, Claude) travel-related questions. AEO is distinct from SEO: instead of ranking for clicks, you're becoming the cited source in an AI-generated answer.

**Why it matters for roammate now:** The primary discovery journey for a travel companion app in 2026 looks like: "Hey Perplexity, what's the best app to find a travel buddy?" or "ChatGPT, how do I meet other travelers in Southeast Asia?" If roammate isn't the answer, a competitor is.
