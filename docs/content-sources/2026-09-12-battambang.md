## Editorial choices

The budget is a September 2026 planning estimate, not a collection of bookable offers. Rows reconcile: budget $30–50, midrange $60–105, splurge $135–245 daily. Midrange accommodation assumes sharing; activities are daily averages. Transfers into/out of town, visas, insurance and premium private tours are excluded.
