roammate is a social travel companion that helps solo and small-group travelers find like-minded people, join excursions, and explore local experiences together.

Whether you're backpacking through Southeast Asia, exploring European cities, or discovering your own neighborhood, roammate connects you with travelers who share your interests and are nearby right now.

KEY FEATURES

Find Travelers Nearby
See who's around you in real-time. Browse profiles, check shared interests, and connect with fellow roamers without the awkward swiping.

Join or Create Excursions
Grab coffee, explore a neighborhood, hit a night market, or go on a hike. Excursions are lightweight, low-commitment activities you can join through the app.

Real-Time Chat
Message other travelers instantly. Share photos, voice messages, and plan meetups. Group and 1-on-1 conversations with typing indicators.

Safety First
Identity verification, emergency SOS with location sharing, check-in timers, and emergency contacts. SOS can alert your configured emergency contacts; it is not a substitute for local emergency services.

Trip Memories
Your excursions, photos, and connections become shareable trip summaries. Relive your adventures and show friends what they're missing.

Build Your Travel Profile
Share your interests, countries visited, languages spoken, and travel style. Your profile is your travel resume.

Bucket List
Track places you want to visit. Discover what other travelers have on their lists. Share completions and inspire each other.

Groups
Join travel communities organized by destination, interest, or travel style. Share tips, plan trips, and find travel buddies.

roammate is for travelers who are currently traveling or about to be. Not a planning tool, not a review site — a live companion for people on the move.
