# Laos standalone guides: editorial reference notes

Research checked 12 September 2026. These notes record evidence, not task tracking.
Both guides use one three-day programme and `generateDerivedPages: false`.
Budgets are explicitly editorial September 2026 planning estimates, not supplier quotes.
Town-day totals exclude guided excursions and major transfers; the itinerary text
does not claim firsthand experience, guaranteed access or guaranteed safety.

## Nong Khiaw

- [Visit Laos Tours transport listings](https://www.visitlaostours.com/ticket-service/): road journey from Nong Khiaw to Luang Prabang advertised at three to four hours; Muang Ngoi private boat options. Published times/prices are not copied as firm schedules or fares. Rail requires a separate road leg.
- [Phou Chalang operator page](https://www.visitlaostours.com/phouchalang-viewpoint/): walking and vehicle access, with a challenging off-road approach. Guide recommends discussing a guided daylight walk rather than hiring an ATV or learning to ride.
- [100 Waterfalls operator itinerary](https://www.visitlaostours.com/tours/1-day-boat-trip-to-100-waterfalls-trek/): full-day boat, village, fields, cascades and return walking format. Inclusion confirmation and weather cancellation are editorial advice; there is no guarantee the published trip operates on a particular date.
- [Luang Prabang tourism overview](https://tourismluangprabang.org/recommended-trips/day-trips/misty-mountains-nong-khiaw-to-muang-ngoi/): destination setting and Muang Ngoi context. Some access wording is older, so it is not used to promise a through river journey from Luang Prabang or to assert that a boat is the only modern access to Muang Ngoi.

## Thakhek

- [Khammouane tourism](https://www.khammouanetourism.org/en): old-town/Mekong base, November to February planning window and Friendship Bridge III connection towards Nakhon Phanom.
- [Official loop overview](https://www.khammouanetourism.org/en/thakhek-loop): approximately 450 km and three to five days. The new guide explicitly distinguishes its three-day town stay from that full motorcycle loop.
- [Nang Aen Cave](https://www.khammouanetourism.org/en/places/tham-nang-aen): named managed cave destination. No unconfirmed hours, prices, swimming recommendation or access guarantee is published.
- [That Sikhottabong](https://www.khammouanetourism.org/en/sikhottabong): Buddhist stupa context. The guide avoids unsupported antiquity, height and entrance-fee claims.
- [SpringRiver Resort](https://www.springriverresort.com/): advertises private transfers from Thakhek towards Kong Lor. Used only to establish a non-rider option to enquire about, not to endorse the operator or promise a daily departure. Kong Lor is an additional overnight extension, not squeezed into the town itinerary.

## Shared official checks

- [Lao eVisa](https://laoevisa.gov.la/): official destination for readers to confirm nationality and checkpoint eligibility. No blanket visa-on-arrival claim.
- [FCDO entry requirements](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/laos/entry-requirements): explicitly British-passport guidance. Avoids presenting changing digital-form checkpoint scope as universally applicable.
- [FCDO safety and security](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/laos/safety-and-security): river/boat risk, rain disruption, unexploded ordnance, riding and passport-deposit precautions. Safety coverage is concise and linked to the full advice.
- [FCDO health](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/laos/health): evacuation cover, limited remote medicine/healthcare, mosquito precautions and air quality.
